# Non-goals

Hoarder deliberately does not do: screen recording (a different product), scrolling capture (no reliable mechanism on Wayland), cross-device History sync, importing History from other managers (cliphist, clipse), pinning a Capture as a floating on-screen window, or a multi-item paste queue. These were considered and dropped to keep the product focused on Capture, Persistence and History; proposals to add them should first reopen this record.
